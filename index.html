Why soft?

Because plenty of us have bitten into a protein bar that fights back.

What we’re aiming for

A bar that’s easy to bite on day one and still easy to bite later. That second part is the hard part, because protein bars tend to firm up as they sit.

What the research says

Glycerol is a common ingredient in soft bars, and the research on it is mixed. One study found it the best of several options for keeping bars soft. Another found it made bars with a lot of whey protein softer at first but hardened them faster. Protein type mattered too. Those were other people’s bars, not ours, so we treat glycerol as something to test, not a fix.

How we’ll find out

Our starting recipe uses a protein blend and glycerol, and it may change. We plan to run blind taste tests and measure firmness across the bar’s shelf life, not just on day one, with other soft bars as comparison. Then we’ll publish what we find, including the bits we don’t like.

Our promise

We’ll label what’s a plan and what’s a result. Anything we haven’t tested stays a “target” until we have, and when the results come in, we’ll show them, even if they’re not what we hoped.

Questions

When can I buy it?

Not yet. The bar is in testing and there is no date or stockist list.

What about allergens?

The draft list is milk, peanuts (Peanut Butter flavour) and oats (cereals containing gluten). It isn’t final. Please always read the pack once there is one.

How much protein?

The target is 20 g per bar. We will confirm it with a lab test of the finished bar before we say it on a pack.

Is it vegan or gluten free?

No. It contains milk and oats.
